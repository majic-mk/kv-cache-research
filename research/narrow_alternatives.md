# Narrow alternatives after rejecting generic scheduling/repair

Updated 2026-10-03. This supplements and supersedes the provisional ranking in `direction_comparison.md`. An independent diagnostic review found that a minimal shared-prefix/deadline scheduling example is solved by a simple one-step shared-aware reservation baseline reaching the oracle. Together with MTDS/Strata/TENT, this kills the current scheduling main. Do not continue it merely by adding an uncertainty margin.

## Provisional main: calibration transfer in heterogeneous reusable caches

**Empirical question:** Does a query-agnostic compressor’s calibration learned from one context region systematically misallocate cache budget to other regions with different content statistics? Can a fixed-compute, multi-region calibration rule recover later evidence without revealing future questions or increasing retained bytes?

The closest primary work is [EchoPress, submitted 2026-09-30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00412). Its [§2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00412v1) constructs an exact-versus-virtual empirical quantile map separately for each layer/KV head using the first reconstruction chunk, then maps later scores through it before global eviction. Its exact reconstruction-input budget is 2,048 tokens. Its stated limitations include task-level code-completion regressions. These details support a concrete hypothesis about calibration transfer, but do not establish that it fails on heterogeneous contexts or that our proposed fix helps. The author-linked repository/branch failed to fetch in this session; do not call it nonexistent or pretend it has been reproduced.

**Why this differs from earlier rejected categories:** the object is a within-context calibration assumption, not I/O scheduling, selecting question-relevant repair tokens, or proposing another generic quantizer. C²KV changes representations for non-prefix composition; KVzap/FastKVzip learn scorers; this candidate tests a training-free score-scale transfer mechanism for a single reusable compressed context. No claim of firstness is justified yet.

**Candidate mechanism to test:** retain the same total reconstruction budget but distribute calibration across context regions. Compare pooled maps, region-conditioned maps with shrinkage toward a global map, and a cheap prefill-only distribution-distance rule that allocates anchors to statistically dissimilar regions. The rule must not see the downstream query, gold answer, or full-KV outcome. A query-agnostic observation of a region’s content is allowed. Keep initial-sink treatment and positional scoring identical across ablations.

### Falsification pilot

0. Start from the paper's existing natural code-completion regression, rather than inventing an adversarial benchmark first. Separate two competing explanations with diagnostic oracles: (a) replace only the regional calibration maps using exact score quantiles while preserving virtual within-region ranks; (b) repair within-region rankings using exact scores while holding allocation fixed. These oracles cost full reconstruction and are *not deployable methods*. If map correction cannot recover a material share of the failure, the calibration hypothesis is wrong and multi-anchor selection should not be built.
1. Then use 32–64 contexts, each 8K–16K tokens, on one supported 7–8B BF16 model. Natural contexts should include code/documentation and multi-document QA; add a separately labeled controlled mix of prose, code, table-like records, and multilingual passages. Use multiple independent questions per shared context, including questions targeting later regions. Fixed permutation variants need their own full-prefill references so ordinary position/order effects are not misattributed to compression.
2. Reproduce full KV, KVzip and paper-faithful EchoPress on the same backend. Use KVpress v0.5.5 as a pinned harness; record an explicit reimplementation label if author code remains unavailable. Do not compare an unoptimized reimplementation’s speed with another paper’s timing.
3. Hold real retained bytes and reconstruction-token budget fixed. Compare first-2048, one random 2048 region, uniformly stratified 4×512 regions, document-boundary-stratified regions, pooled map, and region-conditioned/shrunk map. Count repeat prompts and attention FLOPs: four short reconstructions are not automatically compute-matched to one long reconstruction.
4. Include KVzap/FastKVzip, ExpectedAttention, KeyDiff, start+recent and random pruning. Their lower overhead may dominate the entire proposal. Full KVzip provides a stronger score reference, not a downstream ground-truth oracle.
5. Diagnose transfer error by layer/head/region, threshold-crossing mistakes, preserved evidence and paired answer accuracy. Globally improved score correlation is insufficient. At 50/75/90% eviction, report all-query quality and later-region worst-group quality with paired uncertainty estimates. Inspect mistakes rather than relying only on aggregate F1.

### Gates

- **Reproduction gate:** if no accessible/reimplementable baseline matches paper-defined scoring on a small sanity set, stop method claims and resolve reproduction first.
- **Existence gate:** kill if first-region calibration is within 1 absolute quality point of KVzip on the intended heterogeneous workload, or if observed differences disappear after accounting for order effects and random seeds. Do not manufacture a hostile-only benchmark and market it as typical traffic.
- **Simple-baseline gate:** if equal-budget uniform anchors solve the failure as well as the proposed conditional policy, stop the adaptive-method claim. A small robustness note or upstream improvement may still be useful, but is not automatically a paper.
- **Mechanism gate:** require paired evidence that the gain comes from correcting cross-region scale transfer, not extra reconstruction work, altered sinks, extra retained bytes, or accidentally query-aware selection.
- **Usefulness gate:** continue only if the result gives at least a 2-point held-out quality improvement at matched bytes/build compute, or reaches the reference-quality operating point at meaningfully lower bytes, with no >1-point natural-workload mean regression. These are chosen project gates, not literature facts. Repeat on a second architecture before claiming generality.

**Feasibility:** no retraining and no serving engine fork are needed for the diagnostic. One model at a time, 8K–16K, batch one is compatible with a 32-GB plan subject to measured peaks. Existing KVzip temporary reconstruction and score buffers must be counted. Potential scientific value is medium only if a clear mechanism survives simple stratification; otherwise this is incremental and should be killed promptly.

**Extra paper-reported pins:** EchoPress Appendix B identifies original KVzip commit `5d84729`, KVzap-MLP model revision `bd5c59178466`, and Fast-KVzip model revision `67ce1265f510`. These are paper-reported identifiers, not independently resolved in this session. Its accuracy and speed paths use different attention backends; our causal comparisons must use one common path. The main author-code fetch remains a reproduction issue.

## Backup: compression-transition path dependence

**Question:** For immutable logical KV content ending in the same format, can append/compaction/restore and mixed-format conversion history change the final tensors enough to matter? The proposed contribution, if a real failure exists, would be a path-independent transition rule or canonical representation policy with bounded storage, not a claim that repeated lossy compression was previously unknown.

Important primary counterevidence: [TensorRT-LLM’s compression development guide](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/developer-guide/kv-cache-compression-development.html), last updated 2026-09-26 at displayed commit `c76f4a8`, explicitly requires testing repeated lossy transforms, page reuse, rewind, and suspend/resume. Its [feature documentation](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/features/kv-cache-compression.html) already distinguishes runtime format from NVFP4 cold-page storage. Lifecycle integration itself is occupied.

**Cheap gate:** read one actual supported codec path and determine whether immutable cold bytes survive reload or whether a lossy output is later re-encoded. Same-format quantization may be idempotent; do not assume cumulative drift. A small tensor-level repeated-roundtrip test can precede model inference. Kill if the real path retains canonical bytes, is numerically idempotent, or merely has a known bug fix. Cross-format paths not supported by the engine cannot be presented as production failures. Only after demonstrating a reachable supported sequence should a 3B/8B end-to-end test compare one-shot compression, repeated conversion, retained canonical bytes, and a proposed bounded-overhead repair/canonicalization scheme. This has lower confidence than the calibration pilot.

## Alternatives inspected and rejected as generic reframings

- Dynamic edits: [PIE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03157), [PatchKV edit recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26219), [budgeted contiguous repair](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17983), and [programmable KV notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17107) make generic “repair edited caches” too broad. A mathematically exact receptive-field invalidation scheme could differ, but mixed global-attention layers sharply limit the plausible compute savings and no convincing runtime headroom was established here.
- Correctness fuzzing: [GRIEF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11202), submitted 2026-05-11, already treats timed concurrent traces, shared cache state and silent output corruption as testing targets. “Build a KV-cache fuzzer” is not a fresh main direction.
- Bit-error protection: [Bit-Flip Vulnerability of Shared KV-Cache Blocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17249), submitted 2026-04-19, already studies persistent/shared corruption and a checksum countermeasure. This is not an unoccupied single-GPU project.

The backup in the original report, reuse-count-aware compression investment, remains a valid empirical cost question but is weaker than the calibration-transfer hypothesis: a cheap KVzap/EchoPress baseline or an ordinary amortization threshold may remove the opportunity. No experiment in this research pass established a positive result for any candidate.
